## Editorial portrait — golden-hour window light

**Provider**: openai · **Model**: gpt-image-2 · **Quality**: low · **Size**: portrait

**Prompt**:
```
Editorial portrait photography, woman with dark wavy hair seated against warm neutral plaster wall, soft golden-hour window light from camera-left, gentle shadow on the right side of her face, looking slightly off-camera. Shot on 85mm at f/1.8, shallow depth of field, natural skin tones, no makeup retouching, subtle film grain.
```

**Notes**: Naming the focal length and aperture (`85mm at f/1.8`) is a reliable shortcut for the editorial portrait look. State light direction explicitly — "from camera-left" beats "good lighting."

## Product shot — top-down ceramic mug

**Provider**: openai · **Model**: gpt-image-2 · **Quality**: low · **Size**: square

**Prompt**:
```
Product photography, single ceramic coffee mug centered on dark oak wood surface, top-down 90-degree camera angle, warm cinematic lighting from upper-left casting a soft shadow to the lower-right of the mug. Shallow depth of field, rich coffee visible inside the mug, subtle steam wisp. Catalog editorial style.
```

**Notes**: "Top-down 90-degree camera angle" forces the orthographic flat-lay look (camera prefers oblique by default). Naming the shadow direction makes the lighting believable.
